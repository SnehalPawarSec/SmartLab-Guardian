SmartLab Guardian

Smart computer-lab energy management and monitoring: real-time occupancy, automatic lights and fans, live energy metering, PC usage tracking and unusual energy-pattern detection.

Staff / HOD Login View Features

Live Monitoring

Occupancy, door, lights, fans, voltage, current, power and every lab PC, updated in real time from Firebase.

Automatic Control

The ESP32 switches lights and the fan when the laptop camera or PIR detects people, and beeps when the lab is crowded. Staff can override from the dashboard.

PC Agent

A small Python program on each lab PC reports CPU, RAM, active/idle state, active app and estimated power.

AI Analytics

Detects unusual energy readings, predicts tomorrow's usage and suggests where energy can be saved.
